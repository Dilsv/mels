ACHIEVE A GLOWING COMPLEXION


Originally launched in March 2013, Touchedbymel began as a passion project with a simple yet powerful mission: to promote self-confidence in women through the art of natural beauty. Over time, this vision has grown into a philanthropic business dedicated to creating meaningful impact. Today, Touchedbymel is more than just a beauty brand – it is a platform for empowerment, supporting women who aspire to gain independence and re-enter the workforce. Through a blend of makeup artistry, personal development coaching, and confidence-building initiatives, the organisation helps women realise their full potential, both personally and professionally.
