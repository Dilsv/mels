About Us

Touchedbymel was born from a lifelong passion for beauty, colour, and the artistry of transformation. With just a few years' experience in the industry, the brand has evolved into a trusted name, known for its meticulous attention to detail and personalised approach. Every client experience is designed to feel exclusive, elegant, and empowering — whether you’re preparing for a special occasion, a photoshoot, or simply indulging in self-care. Using premium products and refined techniques, I specialise in creating bespoke looks that enhance your natural features while reflecting your individual style. At the heart of Touchedbymel is a deep commitment to helping clients feel confident, radiant, and unapologetically themselves. From the initial consultation to the final touch-up, the experience is more than makeup — it’s about celebrating beauty, passion, and personal empowerment in every form.
